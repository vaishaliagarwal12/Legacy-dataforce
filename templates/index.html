Phase 4 Active REST Modules & Safe Deletion Policy

Students Module /api/students

CRUD, Roll/Email uniqueness, phone numbers via separate table, multi-field search, branch & CGPA filters.

Courses Module /api/courses

Catalog CRUD, semester & credits validation, enrolled students listing, protected delete if students registered.

Registrations Module /api/registrations

M:N student-course enrollment, year & grade updates, duplicate enrollment prevention, safe unenrollment.

Profiles & Phones /api/profiles & phones

Projects, hackathons, certifications, coding profiles, dedicated separate-table phone management.

🛡️ Career History Preservation (Safe Deletion)

Student deletions are strictly checked against PLACEMENT and INTERNSHIP tables. If a student has any placement offers or completed internships, deletion is blocked with HTTP 409 Conflict to maintain institutional compliance and historical reporting integrity.

Phase 7 Academic & Internship Policy Engine

Semester Results /api/semester-results

Official academic records (SGPA, total/earned credits) enforced with UNIQUE(Student_ID, Semester).

Internship Requirements /api/internships/requirements

Requirement 1: Sem 3 SGPA (Summer after 2nd yr); Requirement 2: Sem 5 SGPA (Summer after 3rd yr).

Prerequisite SGPA Join /api/internships/analytics/prerequisite-sgpa

Dynamically joins INTERNSHIP → REQUIREMENT → SEMESTER_RESULT. Zero redundant SGPA storage (3NF).

Missing Mandatory Audit /api/internships/analytics/missing-mandatory

Identifies active students missing Mandatory Internship #1, #2, or both for graduation clearance.
